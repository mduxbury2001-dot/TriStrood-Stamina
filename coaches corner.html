Monthly Coach's Tip

Lowering expectations is the solution to long-term performance results

We all have days when are legs don't want to move, our mental state is all over, and session execution is simply not where it could be when feeling our best. Remember, when you are at your 50% best, and giving all that 50% in the session, you are still giving your full 100% that day. Focus on consistency, enjoy the process, be kind to yourself and trust the results will come.
