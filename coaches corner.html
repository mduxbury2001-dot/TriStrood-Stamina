Our Coaching Philosophy

At TriStrood Stamina we believe that triathlon should be welcoming, enjoyable and accessible to everyone. Our coaching approach focuses on long-term progression, athlete wellbeing and developing confidence across swimming, cycling and running.

Monthly Coach's Tip

Consistency Beats Perfection

Three good training sessions every week will produce better long-term results than attempting an unsustainable training schedule. Focus on consistency, enjoy the process and trust the results will come.
