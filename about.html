About TriStrood-Stamina

From London to Strood in Rochester, TriStrood-Stamina was founded in 2027 to bring the Medway community closer to Triathlon.

We are a club welcoming all ages and abilities to join and be part of a group of like-minded individuals enthusiastic in interested in swim-bike-run

The club was founded by the duo Head Coach Molly and Coach Claudio from a passion of delivering evidence-backed sport sessions and researched performance

Our motto's pivot from the challenging, self-disciplined mantra of "your hard is nothing" to comforting, self-caring perspective of "your easy is everything" reflects our training philosophy

the philosophy which the club invests itself into is one focusing on consistency, wellbeing, and pushing your limits above what your mind and body believe

you will see this philosophy in our coaching mindset, club attitude, and practiced club training session led by coaches affiliated to the British Triathlon Federation.
