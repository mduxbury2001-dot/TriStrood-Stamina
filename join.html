Join the club

Interested in giving us a 'Tri'?

Whether you're an experienced athlete or completely new to triathlon, you'll find a warm welcome at TriStrood Stamina.

Our club is built around supporting athletes of all abilities to enjoy swimming, cycling and running while developing confidence, fitness and performance.

No previous triathlon experience is required. We welcome juniors and adults who are looking to train, race or simply keep active in a friendly and encouraging environment.

Why Join?

As a member of TriStrood Stamina, you'll benefit from:

✅ Weekly coached training sessions

✅ British Triathlon qualified coaching support

✅ Structured swim, bike and run sessions

✅ Strength and conditioning training

✅ Club social events and race opportunities

✅ Training advice and guidance

✅ A friendly and supportive community
